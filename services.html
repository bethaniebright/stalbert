Our Services

Comprehensive mental health and primary care under one roof, personalized to you and your whole family.

Mental Health

Your Mental Health is our priority

We offer comprehensive mental health care, including psychiatry, treatment planning, and therapy. We specialize in addressing a wide range of mental health conditions, providing personalized support and guidance to help patients improve their well-being.

  • Anxiety, depression & mood disorders
  • Trauma-informed therapy
  • Psychiatric evaluation & medication management
  • Personalized treatment planning

Primary Care

From preventive care to checkups

From preventive care and checkups, to immunizations and exams, our primary care physicians and providers work to keep you and your whole family healthy and strong each and every day.

  • Annual physicals & wellness visits
  • Preventive screenings & immunizations
  • Chronic condition management
  • Treatment of everyday illnesses

Geriatric Therapy

Specialized support for older adults

Specialized mental health support tailored to the unique needs of older adults, addressing concerns like grief, isolation, cognitive changes, and life transitions. We work closely with patients and families to promote dignity, connection, and emotional resilience in later life.

  • Grief & loss counseling
  • Support for isolation & life transitions
  • Cognitive change support
  • Family-centered care

MAT

Medication-Assisted Treatment

Evidence-based treatment combining FDA-approved medication with counseling and behavioral support to help patients recover from opioid or alcohol use disorder. Our compassionate, judgment-free approach is designed to support long-term recovery and improved quality of life.

  • FDA-approved medication
  • Counseling & behavioral support
  • Opioid & alcohol use disorder
  • Judgment-free, long-term recovery focus

SPRAVATO®

Certified SPRAVATO® Providers

SPRAVATO® (esketamine) is an FDA-approved nasal spray for adults with treatment-resistant depression, as well as depressive symptoms in adults with major depressive disorder. As certified providers, we deliver this therapy in a monitored, supportive clinical setting alongside an oral antidepressant.

  • FDA-approved esketamine nasal spray
  • For treatment-resistant depression
  • Administered & monitored on-site
  • Paired with an oral antidepressant
